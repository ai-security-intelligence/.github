# AI Security Intelligence

**Standards organization measuring AI exposure across enterprises, sectors, and markets.**

We develop open, versioned standards for measuring the observable surface area an entity presents to AI-driven risk — and publish continuous, cohort-scoped observations of what that surface looks like across markets.

---

## Standards we publish

- **AI Exposure Standard (AIES)** — the open, versioned methodology defining exposure dimensions, signal catalogs, aggregation rules, and reading protocols.
- **AI Exposure Rating (AIER)** — the capability that produces calibrated readings of an entity's AI exposure per AIES.
- **AI Insurance Readiness Score (AIRS)** — quantifies an organization's AI security posture across five domains and twenty-five discrete factors, producing a composite score with tier classification for insurance underwriting and portfolio-level risk quantification.
- **Vulnerability Scoring Standard (VSS)** — scores individual AI-relevant vulnerabilities; feeds signal inputs into TSS and AIRS.
- **Threat Severity Standard (TSS)** — classifies severity of AI-related threats and incidents on a consistent scale for cross-entity and cross-time comparison.

## Publications

- **The Exposure Bulletin** — monthly cohort-scoped exposure observations. One cohort per issue. Inaugural issue: August 2026 — the Financial Sector Cohort.
- **State of AI Exposure** — annual comprehensive year-in-review. First edition: September 2026.
- **Global AI Exposure Index** — live comparative-rankings data product, continuously refreshed on the portal.
